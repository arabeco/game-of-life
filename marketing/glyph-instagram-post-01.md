# GLYPH Instagram Post 01

Concept:
- Dark luxury layout with gold glow, central symbol and short headline.
- Designed as a single feed post that can also serve as slide 1 of a carousel.

Headline:
- FORJE SUA PROXIMA VERSAO.

Microcopy:
- Evolucao com proposito. Link na bio.

Caption options:
- Sua rotina nao precisa ser comum. No Glyph, progresso vira sistema, e sistema vira legado.
- Menos ruido. Mais foco. Mais evolucao. Descubra o Glyph.
- Transforme habitos em avanco com uma experiencia premium, escura e feita para quem quer ir alem.

Sources used for the creative direction:
- Socialinsider 2025 benchmarks indicate carousel posts still lead engagement on Instagram.
- Adobe Express safe-zone guidance supports keeping key text and logo inside protected visible areas.
- Adobe branding guidance reinforces clarity and legibility as priority in branded design.
- Sprout Social content ideas support educational, myth-busting, infographic and transformation style posts for brand growth.
